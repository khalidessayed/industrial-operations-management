# Industrial Operations & Manufacturing Excellence 🏭

A comprehensive showcase of operational frameworks, production planning methodologies, cost control structures, and performance dashboards built over **30+ years of industrial leadership** across steel fabrication, scaffolding, formwork, and heavy engineering systems.

---

## 🏆 Key Career Achievements & Operational Milestones

### 1. Scaled Factory Operations & Capacity Leadership
* **High-Volume Manufacturing (Saudi Arabia):** Directed full-scope factory operations at **Bin Hindi Factory**, managing a multidisciplinary workforce of **~200 employees** and delivering an average monthly production value of **~SAR 10 Million** across scaffolding, formwork, and steel fabrication.
* **Turnaround & Plant Management (Egypt):** Led day-to-day manufacturing at **Manarat Al Omran** (~95 employees), achieving an average production output of **~800 tons/month** while streamlining fabrication, welding, cutting, and automated coating lines.

### 2. Operational Excellence & Lean Implementation
* **Scrap & Waste Minimization:** Applied Lean principles (5S, Gemba, Root Cause Analysis) and Six Sigma tools to optimize steel utilization, reduce rework, and eliminate fabrication bottlenecks.
* **Preventive Maintenance & OEE:** Implemented scheduled preventive maintenance regimens, significantly increasing equipment availability and cutting unplanned downtime across automated production lines.

### 3. Integrated Cost Control & Production Planning (MRP)
* **Comprehensive Cost Visibility:** Established robust job-order costing, variance analysis, and Bill of Quantities (BOQ) controls at **Arab Mechanical Engineers**, providing top management with real-time budget tracking.
* **Procurement & Supply Chain Alignment:** Aligned material requirements planning (MRP) with factory schedules, mitigating material shortages and securing bulk raw material deliveries on time.

### 4. Engineering Standardization & Product Development
* **Standardized Technical Offices:** Led the engineering and technical office at **Acrow Misr**, creating centralized CAD libraries, standardizing shop drawings, and establishing unified BOQ calculation methods.
* **R&D & Product Launch:** Managed end-to-end product lifecycles at **Arab Mechanical (ULMA Agent)**, designing, prototyping, and deploying proprietary scaffolding and heavy-duty racking systems.

### 5. Business Intelligence & Executive Reporting
* Built interactive **Power BI Dashboards** integrating factory KPIs, OEE, labor productivity, and scrap metrics for C-level visibility and data-driven decision-making.
