# Industrial Operations & Manufacturing Excellence 🏭

A comprehensive repository showcasing operational frameworks, production planning methodologies, cost control structures, and performance dashboards built over 30+ years of factory management in steel fabrication, scaffolding, and formwork industries.

---

## 📌 Core Domains Covered
* **Production Planning & MRP:** Material Requirements Planning, shop-floor scheduling, and capacity planning.
* **Cost Control & Estimation:** Job-order costing, Bill of Quantities (BOQ), and variance analysis.
* **Lean Manufacturing & Operational Excellence:** 5S workplace organization, waste reduction, bottleneck analysis, and Six Sigma principles.
* **Plant & Workforce Management:** Directing operations across facilities with 200+ employees and multi-million SAR monthly outputs.

---

## 📊 Analytics & KPI Frameworks
* **Power BI Dashboards:** Production output tracking, machine downtime, scrap rate monitoring, and OEE.
* **Material Utilization Models:** Maximizing raw material yields in heavy steel fabrication.
